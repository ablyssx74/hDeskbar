# hDeskbar v0.24.0

## New
- **The header is in the SnakeTracker color and fades towards the edge of the screen.** It is raised and shaded like the program tabs and the trail of the menus, and the 30% nearest the menu keeps its color, then melts into the panel's, as the Haiku Deskbar does. It used to fade from top to bottom.
- **The fade follows the side the bar is on.** A bar on the left of the screen fades to the left (the program tabs did the same fade from left to right whatever the side).
- **The header's menu joins its header better.** In a bar at the side of the screen the trail has a rounded inner corner where it leaves the header: at the top for a bar at the top of the screen, and at the bottom for one at the bottom, where the menu's corner next to the header is now square and the far one round.

## Install
From the `linux` folder: `makepkg -si`
